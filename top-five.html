Top Five Goals Towards Financial Freedom

  1. Create a Budget: Track where every dollar goes.
  2. Manage Debt: Focus on high-interest balances first.
  3. Build a Savings: Aim for 3-6 months of expenses.
  4. Invest for the Future: Let compound interest work for you.
  5. Build a Portfolio: Diversify your investment knowledge.
